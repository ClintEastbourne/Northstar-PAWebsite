NORTH STAR SUPPORT WEBSITE REDESIGN

Files:
- index.html: responsive one-page website
- styles.css: visual design and responsive layout
- script.js: mobile navigation
- thank-you.html: Netlify Forms success page
- assets/: the two user-supplied photos optimised to WebP

DEPLOYING TO YOUR EXISTING NETLIFY/GITHUB SITE
1. Download and extract north-star-support-website.zip.
2. Keep a backup of your current website repository before replacing files.
3. Copy index.html, styles.css, script.js, thank-you.html and the assets folder into the root of the repository that Netlify deploys. Commit and push the changes to GitHub.
4. In Netlify, open the site and check Deploys. Wait for the new deploy to finish, then test the live site on a phone and desktop.
5. In Netlify, go to Forms and confirm form detection is enabled. Trigger a fresh deploy if you have just enabled it. Submit a test enquiry and confirm it appears in Forms and that form notifications are configured under site settings.
6. Check the custom domain and HTTPS still work after deployment.

IMPORTANT:
- This package does not deploy anything by itself and does not change your live site until you upload/commit it.
- Google Maps link, telephone and email links are included.
- Contact form uses Netlify Forms. Configure notification emails in Netlify and submit a test before relying on it.
- No claim of insurance, accreditation, or DBS status is made on the page. Only add those claims once confirmed and accurate.
- The site describes the service as non-medical personal assistance and explicitly states personal care/nursing/medical care are not offered.
